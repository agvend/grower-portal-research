# Phase 2 Planning: Grower Portal Research with Partner Growers

*Draft planning doc — Sep 2026. Purpose: turn Phase 1 (partner/staff interviews) findings into a concrete plan for interviewing growers directly. This is a starting point for team discussion, not a finalized plan.*

## Why this phase, and why now

Phase 1 talked to partner-side staff (agronomy, grain, accounting) across 10 ag retailers about how *their customers* use the portal. That's proxy feedback. Daniel flagged this explicitly back on Aug 3: "I had pictured that we share our findings with partners as part of the ask to talk to farmers." Several Slack threads (Aug 19, Sep 16) show the team already treating this as the natural next step, and it's the reason Jeff is building the shareable findings page — partners need to see we've done real work before they'll open the door to their growers.

## Sequencing: share findings first, then ask for grower access

1. Finish and share the participant-facing findings summary (the web page this project is also producing) with all 10 study participant companies — a commitment already made in-channel (Aug 5, 2026).
2. Use that share as the vehicle for the ask: "here's what we learned from your team, now can we talk to a few of your growers?"
3. Prioritize outreach through partners who were most engaged/positive in Phase 1 (e.g., River Valley Coop, Heritage Cooperative) as easier wins, while still covering partners with more friction (NEW Coop/Control) to get contrast.

## What to carry forward and validate directly with growers

Phase 1 surfaced staff-reported hypotheses. Phase 2's job is to check which of these actually match grower experience and priority — staff and growers don't always agree on what matters. Candidate hypotheses to test:

- **Grain offer/contract visibility & notifications** — do growers actually want a notification when an offer converts to a contract, or is this staff's problem, not theirs?
- **Multi-user / shared-account friction** — is the "one login per household/operation" model actually a pain point for growers, or a workaround they've made peace with? Ask about who else in the operation uses the portal, and how.
- **Payments/ACH trust** — do growers actually distrust balance/notification reliability, or is that a staff-side proxy for something else (e.g., their own reconciliation headaches)?
- **Discoverability vs. missing features** — for the "why can't I do X on the fuel side" complaints, test whether growers know the feature exists elsewhere, and whether cross-business-line consistency actually matters to them.
- **Loyalty/rewards program clarity** — confirmed discoverability issue in one case; worth a quick check across more growers.
- **Mobile experience** — Phase 0 survey data (2022–2025) already shows strong, sustained mobile demand; worth confirming this is still the top ask and probing which specific tasks growers most want on mobile.

## Methodology recommendation: pair open discovery with concept tests

Phase 1's retro (Jul 20) found that a pure open-questions approach ("what would you change?") under-elicits feedback from satisfied users — SWT's interviews produced little critical signal. For Phase 2:

- Keep 1–2 open discovery questions to catch anything unanticipated.
- Add 3–4 **concrete concept probes** grounded in Phase 1 findings — e.g., show a mockup of an offer-to-contract notification, or describe a shared-household login with split permissions, and ask growers to react.
- Ground those probes in the Phase 1 themes in `03-interview-synthesis.md` (offers, shared logins, payments trust, discoverability).

## Persona / sampling considerations

- Aim for growers who span: primary portal users vs. occasional/delegated users (relevant to the multi-user theme), different farm operation structures (solo vs. multi-generational/multi-user households), and a mix of grain-heavy vs. diversified (energy, agronomy) accounts.
- Within partners already interviewed, prioritize pulling growers from accounts where staff flagged specific friction (e.g., a NEW Coop grower who's hit the notification gap) so we can validate concrete incidents, not just general sentiment.
- Consider 2–3 growers per partner, across a smaller set of partners (5–6) rather than spreading thin across all 10, given scheduling difficulty already experienced in Phase 1 (RVC and others were hard to pin down).

## Logistics / open items for the team

- Who owns partner outreach for grower access — PSMs again, or does the shared findings page make this a more direct ask from the research team?
- Recording/consent process for grower calls — same Granola/Fireflies setup as Phase 1, but confirm growers are comfortable being recorded (they're a different audience than partner staff).
- Timeline: Phase 1 took roughly 10 weeks from kickoff (Jun 22) to ~14 completed interviews with slowdowns around scheduling. Build in the same buffer, and treat the mid-phase "did we lose momentum" moment as a known risk (assign a single owner to track scheduling weekly rather than ad hoc).
- Confirm whether this phase also needs Partner Success / Sales sign-off on which specific growers get contacted, mirroring the Phase 1 partner-selection process.

## Open questions for team discussion

- Should Phase 2 include any partners *not* in the original 10, to widen ERP/geography coverage further?
- What's the minimum number of grower interviews needed before we treat a hypothesis as validated vs. just "worth prototyping"?
