### Grower relationship philosophy

- Top performers have the strongest grower relationships, enabling two-way feedback.
- Key coaching point: avoid being a “puppy dog,” push back when needed.
- In-person visits remain the foundation. Brian nearly missed the call due to a farm visit.

### Portal value and adoption

- Portal drives transparency: growers can see invoices, bookings, and purchases in real time. Catches errors and creates more touchpoints.
- Cendak came from Bushel. AgVend is “100x better” for agronomy use.
- Adoption deliberately slowed to let the team get comfortable before adding more.
- Product Management added this summer: biggest impact of any add-on. Moving away from Excel for positions and sales tracking this fall.
- Shared view between staff and growers is a standout. Staff can walk growers through the portal in real time over the phone.

### CRM and notes

- CRM Plus not yet adopted. Price tag was a significant barrier.
- Notes used informally for cross-sell leads (e.g. flagging a grower unhappy with their fuel supplier). Functional but not used consistently.
- Sees CRM value mainly as an internal tool: tracking salesperson activity, customer history on rep transfers.

### Feedback and gaps

- Notifications for invoices, bookings, and contracts are inconsistent and cumbersome for bookkeeping staff. Top pain point.
- Business Intelligence reports cannot filter by customer or family of growers, only by product. Workaround is checking each account individually.
- Program Management seen as promising but not ready at conference. Gap: cannot calculate blended average price when carry-over inventory mixes with new purchases. Others in the room shared the same concern.
- Grain portal: Brian has no visibility into it.

### Seasonality and close

- Busy season roughly April 25 to early August. No appetite for tool changes in that window. Fall through spring is the window for evaluation and add-ons.
- Strongest endorsement: AgVend’s support team is the top differentiator vs previous vendors that required tickets with slow or no resolution.
- Grower adoption described as really good overall.

### Action items from Granola

- Investigate notification improvements for invoices, bookings, and contracts.
- Review Business Intelligence filtering by customer or grower family.
