### Product Feature Gaps and Enhancement Opportunities

Small but impactful feature improvements are needed to boost user satisfaction and business value (15:55).

Improved communication tools inside the portal are a high priority (14:22). The portal is underused for targeted messaging to small groups of growers. Current news tile only links to an external webpage, lacking rich content options. Dan wants the ability to share images, videos, or podcasts directly in the portal. Enhanced messaging could support event invites, field updates, and timely info sharing.

Harvest hours tracking needs a simple, user-friendly tile (16:25). Currently requires manual data entry at 85 locations daily, which is inefficient. A tile that remembers preferred locations and lets farmers quickly log hours is desired. This feature existed in the old platform but regressed in the Agvend portal transition. Improving this would reduce admin burden and improve grower engagement during harvest.

Voice commands and custom queries could significantly boost adoption (22:46). Dan envisions a “goose” feature where farmers can ask for key metrics verbally. Examples include bushels delivered, gallons purchased, and equity held. Allowing farmers to create and save custom data queries would increase portal usage. This kind of innovation would differentiate the portal and drive sustained engagement.

Integration with vehicle tracking and program sign-ups can add value (22:46). A live vehicle tracking link showing deliveries in progress would improve transparency. The portal could enable farmers to sign up for programs (e.g., LCFS) by selecting fields visually. These features would streamline operations and reduce duplicate data entry. Dan sees these as potential game changers that would justify additional investment.

### User Experience and Interface Improvements

The portal’s navigation and dashboard require simplification and better organization to meet grower expectations (10:45).

Dashboard not intuitive, especially on the customer portal side (10:45). Dan highlighted that the portal’s original design focused on team hub, not growers. The current dashboard lacks clear, user-friendly layout tailored for farmer needs. Important revenue streams like agronomy were historically underrepresented. Efforts to add agronomy tiles are progressing but missing key functions like contract prepays.

Fragmentation across tiles leads to excessive clicking and confusion (12:53). Users must jump between agronomy, grain, and other tiles for related tasks. Lack of consistency with tiles (e.g., CFA balances shown but no tile exists) confuses users. Dan stressed the need to consolidate related features into their respective tiles. This would reduce frustration and create a more seamless user journey.

Team hub dashboard has irrelevant features dominating space (25:53). Reports and leaderboards occupy too much dashboard real estate. Dan suggests replacing these with more relevant business info at first glance. This feedback indicates a need to rethink information hierarchy for better focus.

Equity information difficult for farmers to find (29:13). Equity balances require multiple clicks through nested menus. Adding a direct equity link on the customer portal would enhance transparency. This is especially important since equity represents farmers’ ownership stake. Dan believes this would be a simple but impactful improvement for all cooperatives.

Open to paying for major portal enhancements if they deliver clear business value (22:06). Dan accepts some add-on charges as the platform grows but wants clear ROI. His feedback will guide Agvend’s investment prioritization in coming months.
