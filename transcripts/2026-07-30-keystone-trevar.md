## Notes

### Portal usage and value

- Trevar uses the Connect app daily, primarily for bookings, quotes, and sales tracking.
- Transparency is the core value: reps and growers see the same data, replacing paper and phone calls.
- Growers use it most for billing history and year-over-year price tracking, job completion notifications (especially dry fertilizer spreading), and seeing field-level activity without calling in.
- Trevar uses it for prospecting: checking if a grower has an assigned rep, logging visit notes, tracking storage and buying style.
- Peak usage: October through February (agronomy sales cycle).
- Blend tickets (fertilizer orders) still built in a separate system (Sky / SSI), not in Connect.

### Grower feedback and requests

- Most-requested: better map functionality. Fields appear in isolation with no road numbers or neighboring context. Wants zoom tied to Google Maps or Apple Maps, usable on mobile roadside. Growers and applicators need to distinguish adjacent fields (e.g. Home Farm vs Troxel) and find driveways. Pins for applicator routing would prevent wrong-driveway incidents. acres.com cited as a reference.
- Two-way field status notifications. Growers want to mark a field as planted and auto-notify the rep to trigger next service. Potential John Deere or CNH integration. Currently phone or text, easy to forget in busy season.
- Load number distribution for product pickup. When growers pick up product (e.g. 28% UAN) from a remote location, reps manually text or email load numbers. Growers and drivers want load numbers in the portal so drivers can self-serve.
