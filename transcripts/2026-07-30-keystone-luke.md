### Role and grower relationship

- Grain merchandiser at Keystone Coop, based in Indianapolis. Covers six eastern Indiana elevators and a feed mill.
- Core value: full farmer footprint visibility across grain, agronomy, and energy.
- Volatile markets make real-time contract visibility critical.
- Age divide: farmers 40 and under move quickly; older farmers adopt slower but do adopt. Example: a non-smartphone farmer now checks contracts daily via a bookmarked browser link with his wife.

### What’s working

- Contract visibility: growers can see contracts, scale tickets, settlements by crop year.
- Removes guesswork vs Excel and pen-and-paper.
- Enables smarter selling: farmers use historical bushel data to set pricing benchmarks.
- Cross-division view (grain, agronomy, energy) gives a near-complete operational picture.

### Gaps and requests

- No offer tracking. Growers cannot see open offers. Luke hears “did my offer from January hit?” regularly. Internal risk: offers fall through when markets move fast.
- Contact info not visible enough. Agronomy or energy customers cannot easily find a grain rep’s direct number.
- Cash bid text alerts send via email, not SMS. Phone on file is often a home or spouse number.
- Cash bid delay (10–15 min) noted but accepted as industry standard.
- Wants futures price charts alongside cash bids.
- Automated contract signing reminders if a contract sits unsigned past X days.
- Contract expiry / roll alerts when a basis or futures contract needs to be priced or rolled.

### AI appetite

- Luke uses ChatGPT for market research. Keystone runs Copilot training. Open to AI-driven alerts or proactive nudges.
- Interested in joining the farmer research round. Prefers usage-based selection over hand-picking, to surface farmers using grain plus agronomy plus energy.
