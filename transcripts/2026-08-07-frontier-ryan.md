## Notes

### Grower relationship and portal role

- Simplicity is Ryan’s top priority. If a process is too hard, growers delegate it back to Frontier staff, creating repetitive low-value work.
- Goal: make the app something growers are excited to use, not scared of.
- One-stop shop is a key differentiator: seed, fertilizer, energy, crop insurance, payments all in one place.
- Switched from Bushel to AgVend primarily to serve non-grain customers (energy, etc.) who had zero value in the old app.
- Frontier Connect now has something for every customer type.

### Grower usage and feature feedback

- Ryan rode with advisors this summer, visiting growers on-farm and asking about the app.
- Top three features by usage: cash bids, scale tickets, offers (roughly 75% of usage). Audience skewed toward field operators, not the person handling invoicing. Billing often delegated to a spouse, sibling, or other family member.
- Most-requested missing feature: field name annotation on scale tickets. Existed in Bushel as a free-text field. Did not sync to ERP, but growers used it to track which field a load came from. Growers previously wrote field names on paper tickets, then entered them into spreadsheets at end of harvest. Grain traceability is growing in importance; possible premium pricing for growers who can provide origin data.
- Overall farm-visit feedback was very positive. No significant negative feedback.

### Priorities and wishlist

- Frontier is focused on better engaging a tier of smaller, more price-sensitive growers they have under-served. Previously leaned too heavily into deep farm planning for larger customers. Plan to use AgVend marketing tools to reach this segment.
- Biggest portal wish: share geospatial and as-applied data directly with growers. Soil sampling, yield, planting, harvest. As-applied maps from John Deere (90% of custom application). See and Spray variability maps (120–130k acres of aerial application). Currently delivered by email as PDFs. Ryan is aware of Keystone / field analytics rumors and is patiently waiting.
- Adoption driver: accounting clerks at locations prompting growers who bring in checks to use the app for payments.
- Competitive landscape: largely similar to peers (CVA uses a comparable app). Frontier slightly ahead on energy / tank monitoring.
