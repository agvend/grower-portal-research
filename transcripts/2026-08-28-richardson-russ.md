## Notes

### Grower relationship philosophy

- “Boots on the ground” is Richardson’s core value-add, with the portal as an enhancement layer, not a replacement for people.
- Russ sees the portal as a one-stop shop for all things Richardson.
- North star: serve 10,000 growers at 25% market share with fewer people, more efficiently.
- Older retail staff (20+ year veterans) see the portal as a threat rather than a resource. Big internal lift to get everyone signed up and trained.
- Live grain pricing was the key “aha moment” that drove grower adoption.

### Inbox strategy and marketing overload

- Top priority: disciplined inbox messaging with true calls to action only. Too many e-blasts and supplier marketing create “ad soup.” Growers start ignoring everything.
- Pilot underway: 200 growers in one location, turning off e-blasts and doubling down on inbox-only offers.
- Marketing tab on the portal (already in development) is the proposed fix. Supplier content, banner ads, and promo live on the tab. Inbox reserved for actionable offers.
- Allows macro (network-wide) and micro (regional, under-the-radar) campaign targeting. Competitive advantage: campaigns can run quietly without tipping off competitors.
- Text messaging is heavily used by staff but lacks standardization across 92 stores. About 50 different approaches. Russ wants this standardized internally (not a platform gap).

### AR, e-commerce, and roadmap

- Accounts receivable visibility is the second biggest ask. Wants outstanding balance, available credit, and Richardson Rewards points on the main landing page. Analogizes to airline loyalty dashboards. Ability to pay down accounts through the portal is also on the wish list (Jared and Nate have discussed with Robo).
- E-commerce is on the roadmap but not urgent for core Canadian markets. Richardson has 25% market share and heavy asset and people investment. Full e-commerce risks commoditizing the offer (FBN / Amazon comparison). Better fit for thinner markets: North Dakota (3–4 stores, durum milling, limited asset base). Would start with crop protection and seed; fertilizer is harder. US portal build for North Dakota already in progress.
- Goose (AI smart suggestions) flagged as the next big evolution. Agronomic recommendations to grow share of acre. Russ wants continued investment as Goose matures.
- Nexus (supplier integration) seen as ongoing work in progress.
