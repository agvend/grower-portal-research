### Brian’s role and portal usage

- Director of Marketing and Communications at Heritage Cooperative. Not heavily customer-facing. Focused on brand protection, grower communications, and marketing.
- Heritage also has an energy division (First Choice Energy Services) with commercial and industrial customers.
- Uses the grower portal primarily as an email hub, mostly for agronomy-side communications.
- Sends internal versions first so sales staff know what customers received before calls come in.
- Moved from the built-in composer to full HTML emails for richer formatting.
- Pushes all emails to a public web page, then links to that page from in-app and text messages.

### Pain points and gaps

- Folder sort order resets on every navigation. Organized by year and month folders; always lands on 2023 folders instead of current year. Requires 3 extra clicks to re-sort. Starring folders is not a useful workaround.
- Composer too limited, forcing the HTML workaround.
- No way to build marketing attribution reports in Business Intelligence. Can see per-email attribution (e.g. $26K in 30-day attributed sales) within the email view, but cannot connect that data to BI to slice by email, channel, or month. Workaround: Microsoft Lists and a custom spreadsheet with automation.
- Branding flexibility: First Choice Energy customers do not recognize Heritage Cooperative. Would want a logo and name swap on the portal and app for energy-only customers. Branding difference is minimal (logo and positioning, not colors or fonts).
- Grower portal navigation feels visually heavy. Tile view gives everything equal visual weight. Hard to quickly find grain, energy, etc. Items like invoices, bookings, purchase history, quotes, and statements look similar at a glance. Bigger concern for older or infrequent users.

### Overall sentiment

- No deal-breakers. Describes issues as minor inconveniences.
- Positive on core functionality: texting, in-app messages, email, and data connectivity.
- Open to grower referrals for phase two if any come to mind.
