- Works with growers on corn and bean marketing, primarily via phone, text, and in person.

### Portal usage

- Primary uses: placing offers, signing contracts, viewing contracts, proof of yield reports, payments (check and ACH), storage visibility.
- Biggest win: growers placing offers independently, especially outside business hours.
- Fall is peak portal season. Activity otherwise tracks market trends.
- Word of mouth driving new signups.
- Pitch: instant access vs waiting 10 days for mail; tickets visible next day after hauling.

### Common grower questions

- Contracts hard to find, especially for new users.

- Entity filter confusion: growers selecting one entity instead of “show all,” missing offers or tickets.
- Contract void alerts: confusing notifications tied to Iowa’s 30-day signature requirement for deferred contracts.
