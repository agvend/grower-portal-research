### About Lisa

- Grain / row crop merchandiser at Heritage Cooperative, Marysville, Ohio (west of Columbus).
- 25 years at Heritage. Involved with AgVend since early adoption, primarily on the grain side.
- Not part of the original AgVend decision, but helped implement it for grain operations.

### How Lisa uses AgVend

- Uses CRM notes to track farmer relationships: acreage, yields, family and business partners.
- Scale tickets in farmer profiles give year-over-year delivery volume.
- Pitches the portal around grain bids, e-signing contracts, contract balance visibility, and offers via StoneX.

### What farmers love

- Cash bids: most-viewed feature. Farmers check constantly.
- StoneX offers: biggest differentiator vs competitors. Farmers place offers throughout the evening. Target offers existed before, but the portal put it in the palm of their hand and drove adoption.
- E-signing and going paperless: well received.
- Settlements and proof of yield are available but underused. Farmers still call Lisa for those.

### Friction and gaps

- Messaging confusion: farmers unsure whether to reply to marketing texts, salesperson texts, or the in-app inbox. Different numbers for marketing vs sales texts cause misdirection. Farmers sometimes think they are talking to Lisa when replying to a marketing blast.
- Inbound email routing: general mailbox (info@heritagecooperative.com) receives unrouted farmer questions. Forwarding is manual and often misdirected (grain vs propane). Lisa suggested a dropdown or checklist so farmers select a department before sending.
- Price charts: a few farmers have asked for 30-day futures or cash bid charts. Likely a StoneX integration question.
- Behavioral signals (started a bid but did not complete): interesting in theory, too much data to act on daily.
